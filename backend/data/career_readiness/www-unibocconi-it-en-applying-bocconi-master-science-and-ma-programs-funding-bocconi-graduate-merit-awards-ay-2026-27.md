# Bocconi Graduate Merit Awards a.y. 2026-27

Bocconi University offers a set of merit-based scholarships, the **Bocconi
Graduate Merit Awards**, addressed to outstanding first-year graduate students
enrolling in a Bocconi Master of Science (MSc) or MA program for the
2026-27 academic year. The awards are funded by Bocconi University's own
resources and private donations.

## Award amount and format

- **Award value**: a **100% waiver of the Bocconi ordinary academic tuition
  and fees** for the academic year of reference.
- **Format**: the award is a **tuition waiver only** — there is no stipend
  or additional monetary component included in the Graduate Merit Award.
- **Coverage**: the waiver applies to the Bocconi ordinary academic tuition
  and fees component of the program cost; it does not cover regional taxes,
  stamp duty or other non-Bocconi charges.

## Eligibility and selection

The Graduate Merit Awards are addressed to incoming first-year graduate
students of any nationality (International, Italian, and current Bocconi
applicants).

- **Automatic consideration**: top academic performers among the
  applicants are automatically considered for the award based on the
  application materials submitted to the Admissions Office.
- **No separate application**: a separate application for the merit award
  is **not required**; selection is based on the regular admission dossier.
- **Selection criteria**: academic achievement is the primary factor,
  evaluated alongside the comprehensive application review.
- **Decision committee**: a committee appointed by the Rector — including
  the Dean of the Graduate School and the Dean for International Affairs —
  determines the number, amount, and recipients of the awards, and
  allocates the awards across admission rounds at its discretion.

## Communication and acceptance

- The award outcome is communicated **together with the admission result**.
- Recipients must complete an **online scholarship acceptance** before the
  enrollment deadline and upload an updated CV (the CV may be shared with
  donors).
- The reserve enrollment must be lifted by **September 30, 2026**.

## Renewal conditions (second year)

The Graduate Merit Award is renewed automatically for the second year of
the program if the recipient:

1. completes the annual enrollment within the deadlines for the second
   year of study, AND
2. has earned a minimum of **50 credits (CFU) by September 10, 2027**.

Awards cannot be renewed during a *fuori corso* (extended-duration) year.

## Revocation

The award is revoked in any of the following cases:

- false statements made during the application process;
- disciplinary sanctions issued by the University;
- failure to remove the enrollment reserve by September 30, 2026;
- acceptance of an equal or larger financial benefit from another source.

## Special provision for double / joint degree students

Students participating in the LSE-Bocconi, Sciences Po-Bocconi, or
Yale-Bocconi double / joint degree programs cannot renew the Graduate
Merit Award for the year spent at the partner institution if the
partner provides a full tuition waiver for that year.

## Related graduate awards

The Bocconi Graduate Merit Award is one of several merit-based and
need-based scholarships available to incoming graduate students. Related
awards listed on the Bocconi funding pages include:

- **Bocconi Award - Artificial Intelligence** — a separate merit-based
  award for students enrolling in selected AI-relevant graduate programs.
- **Cyber Scholarship / Cyber Partnership Scholarship** — applicable to
  the MSc in Cyber Risk Strategy and Governance, the joint degree with
  Politecnico di Milano.
- **Bocconi4Access to Education** — need-based support for graduate
  applicants with limited financial means.
- **ISU Bocconi scholarship** — regional-tax-driven need-based support.

For the canonical descriptions of each related award, see the Bocconi
Master of Science and MA programs funding pages.
